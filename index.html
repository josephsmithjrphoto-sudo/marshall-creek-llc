Privacy Policy

Marshall Creek LLC, doing business as Marshall Mercantile
Last updated: October 27, 2026

What this tool is

Marshall Mercantile is a small resale business that sells on eBay. We use a private software tool, connected through eBay's developer APIs, to manage our own eBay listings: titles, categories, item specifics and descriptions. The tool is used only by the business owners. It is not offered to the public or to other eBay members.

What we collect

The tool reads and updates listing information for our own eBay seller account. It does not collect, request or store personal information about buyers or any other eBay user, and it does not collect information from visitors to this page. We do not use cookies or analytics on this page.

How we use it

Listing information is used only to improve and maintain our own listings. We do not sell, rent or share it with anyone.

Sign-in

We sign in to eBay through eBay's own authorization page. We never see or store an eBay password. Authorization can be revoked at any time from our eBay account settings.

Account deletion notices

Because the tool does not store eBay user data, it has been exempted from eBay's Marketplace Account Deletion notification requirement.
